# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Standard

We expect contributors to be direct, respectful, and focused on the work.
Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels
- giving and receiving constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes
- prioritizing the health of the community and project

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, or publishing private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict access for
behavior that violates this code. Enforcement should be proportional, documented,
and focused on restoring a safe working environment.

## Reporting

Report issues privately to the maintainers listed in the repository metadata.
Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing reports and will
protect reporter privacy as much as practical.
